Stack takes the stack live: every fader goes to zero and the first step starts. Up: seconds the fader takes to its value. Wait: with 0 the stack stays on the step until Play; with more it goes on by itself after that many seconds. Down: seconds the fader takes back to zero when the stack leaves the step. After the last step comes the first. A step keeps its number when another is moved; type a number to change it.
