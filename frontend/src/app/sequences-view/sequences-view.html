Speed {{ speedText() }}

Up: seconds from zero to the value. Wait: seconds it stays; with 0 the next step starts at once. Down: seconds back to zero when a step takes it down. Add: the step comes up on top of what is on; the next step without Add takes everything down, and so does every new round. Release takes one step down, in its own down time. Effect plays an effect as strongly as its step is up, Sound a sound as loud; with Continue the sound plays on through the steps after it, also into a new round, until its end or a release. The steps that are on are marked green. Disabled skips a step without taking it out, also while the stack is live. Random plays all steps on every round, each round in a new order. The speed divides every time: 2× is twice as fast. Rec seq in the main view adds steps by pressing keys.
