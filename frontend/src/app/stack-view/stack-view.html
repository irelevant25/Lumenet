No steps yet. Add step puts in the first one.

Stack takes the stack live: every fader goes to zero and the first step starts. Up: seconds the fader takes to its value. Wait: with 0 the stack stays on the step until Play; with more it goes on by itself after that many seconds. Down: seconds the fader takes back to zero when the stack leaves the step. A light that both steps give at the same level stays up; the others rise with the new step's up time and fall with the old step's down time. Add: the step comes up on top of what is on and takes nothing down; the next step without Add takes everything down that it does not give itself. Release takes one step that is on down, in its own down time, and leaves the rest. The steps that are on are marked green. After the last step comes the first. A step keeps its number when another is moved; type a number to change it. + puts a new step right after its row.
